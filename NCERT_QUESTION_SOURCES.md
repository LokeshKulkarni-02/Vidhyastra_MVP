# NCERT syllabus sources for Vidhyastra Maths and Science quizzes

The chapter titles and broad themes in this expansion were checked against current NCERT 2026–27 textbook listings and curriculum pages. Question prompts, distractors and explanations are original Vidhyastra practice content, not verbatim copies of textbook exercises or commercial question banks.

## Official portals and copyright

- NCERT textbook portal: https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php
- CBSE 2026–27 curriculum: https://cbseacademic.nic.in/curriculum_2027.html
- NCERT Class 9 syllabus hub: https://ncert.nic.in/syllabus_nep.php

NCERT's textbook portal states that its textbooks are copyrighted and that republication or use as part of digital content packages/software is not permitted without written permission. Vidhyastra therefore links to the official textbooks and uses original questions based on published chapter themes; it does not embed textbook exercise questions, paragraphs, illustrations or answer keys.

## Scope and preserved questions

The new JSON adds **100 chapter assessments / 2,000 questions**:

- Class 7 Mathematics: all 8 chapters.
- Class 8 Mathematics: Chapters 2–14, preserving the existing Chapter 1 bank.
- Class 9 Mathematics: all 14 chapters.
- Class 10 Mathematics: all 14 chapters.
- Science Classes 7–10: all 12, 13, 13 and 13 chapters respectively.

Class 6 Mathematics already has 10 chapter quizzes and Class 6 Science already has 12 chapter quizzes. Class 8 Mathematics Chapter 1 retains its chapter assessment and the original Square Numbers and Cubic Numbers drills. No existing question records are replaced.

Each new chapter has one assessment with exactly 20 items: 8 easy, 8 medium and 4 hard. The school should confirm that its prescribed board, language and edition match the NCERT chapter mapping before assigning these as formal assessments.
