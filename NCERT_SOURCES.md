NCERT says its textbooks are copyrighted and prohibits republication and use of the online textbooks as part of digital content packages or software. Vidhyastra must link to official textbook resources and write original questions. Do not copy textbook paragraphs, exercise questions, illustrations or answer-key text into Vidhyastra.
