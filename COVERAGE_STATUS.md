## Required rule for each future chapter

One chapter = one assessment topic = exactly 20 distinct questions:

- 8 easy
- 8 medium
- 4 hard

Each question must have a unique ID, four unique options, exactly one answer present in the options, and an explanation. Questions must be original, mapped to the correct class/subject/chapter, and reviewed for correctness and age appropriateness.

## Board / edition limitation

The app currently accepts multiple education boards. This package adds NCERT Class 6 Science content, not official syllabus content for ICSE, IB, Cambridge or every State Board. Check the student's prescribed edition before making that content their assessed curriculum.
